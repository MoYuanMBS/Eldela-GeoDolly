# GeoDolly Use and Data Policy / GeoDolly 使用与数据政策

The English policy text controls if the Chinese translation differs. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) contains the binding Software use restrictions and the project artwork and brand terms. This policy explains those restrictions and describes the intended scope of any future official hosted service; it does not expand the license grant.

## English

### Research, public use, and commercial activity

Personal study, education, independent academic work, nonprofit and public-interest work, and public-sector work and research are permitted when they comply with the LICENSE. Military academies and research institutions may conduct historical, methodological, or other non-operational research. Government or grant funding alone does not remove this permission. Calling an activity research does not permit an operational military or commercial purpose.

Commercial products, subscriptions, paid hosting, paid GIS analysis or consulting, commercial client deliverables, and internal business use intended to support commercial operations require separate written permission. Free access to a commercial service does not make its use noncommercial.

### Military, political, and sensitive geographic work

Do not use GeoDolly to plan or support real-world military operations, select or assess targets, gather operational reconnaissance or intelligence, guide troop movements, or plan operational supply and logistics. This includes providing GeoDolly results to another party for those purposes. Historical study and non-operational teaching are permitted only while they remain separate from such activity.

Lawful political, historical, and geographic research is permitted. Do not conduct or knowingly facilitate political propaganda or incitement, broadcasting or communications activity, separatist incitement, or another political activity where that activity is unlawful under applicable local law. A disputed place name or boundary does not by itself make research prohibited.

If an official GeoDolly service is offered, it will not intentionally provide dedicated datasets or analytical overlays purporting to establish disputed sovereignty or administrative boundaries, lines of actual control, conflict front lines, or armed-group control areas. It will not claim to infer authoritative versions of these datasets from ordinary map features. This is a rule for official service content, not a determination of any territory's legal status. Users of their own copies may study lawful, user-supplied or external data under the LICENSE and third-party terms; GeoDolly makes no promise that its built-in tools will fetch, infer, or render such sensitive datasets.

### External data, attribution, and retention

GeoDolly can query OpenStreetMap-related place and feature services and load raster basemaps from configured providers. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL); basemap imagery, provider metadata, and other external data may have separate terms. The GeoDolly LICENSE does not relicense them or remove attribution duties. Third-party tiles and upstream records may themselves show disputed names or boundaries. Their presence is not an official GeoDolly determination or endorsement.

Map views, screenshots, exports, and redistributed results must retain the attribution required by their data and tile providers. Users and deployers must follow provider terms, rate limits, and applicable access and caching rules; they must not evade access controls or conceal required attribution. See [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), the [OpenStreetMap tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/), and each configured provider's terms.

External place, feature, and tile requests send the information needed for those requests, such as a search query, map area, or tile coordinates, to the configured provider. Its own privacy and usage terms apply. A self-hosted operator controls provider choices, access logs, and retention. An official hosted service needs a separate privacy and retention notice before public launch; this file is not that notice.

### Official service controls

A separately hosted or modified copy is not an official GeoDolly service. If an official service is launched, its operator may maintain private Core Policy, Restricted Data Config, Jurisdiction Policy, and Official Internal Config outside this public repository and enforce service restrictions in server-side code. Public prompts and configuration can be edited by users and are not security boundaries. This describes an intended separation; it does not represent that private controls are already deployed. The LICENSE governs Software copies independently of hosted service rules.

### 研究、公共用途与商业活动

遵守 `LICENSE` 的个人学习、教育、独立学术研究、非营利及公益工作、公共部门工作和研究均被允许。军事院校和研究机构可以开展历史、方法论或其他非作战研究。政府或研究经费本身不改变这项许可。将活动称为“研究”，不能使作战或商业目的变成允许用途。

商业产品、订阅服务、收费托管、收费 GIS 分析或咨询、商业客户交付物，以及旨在支持商业经营的企业内部使用，均需另行取得书面许可。商业服务即使向终端用户免费开放，也不因此成为非商业用途。

### 军事、政治与敏感地理研究

不得使用 GeoDolly 规划或支持现实军事行动、选择或评估目标、开展作战侦察或情报搜集、指导部队行动，或规划作战补给与后勤；也不得向他人提供 GeoDolly 结果以实现这些目的。历史研究和非作战教学仅在与此类活动保持独立时被允许。

合法的政治、历史及地理研究被允许。不得开展或明知而协助在适用的当地法律下违法的政治宣传或煽动、广播或通信活动、分裂煽动及其他政治活动。研究对象涉及争议地名或边界，本身不构成禁用理由。

如未来提供 GeoDolly 官方服务，该服务不会有意提供声称确立争议主权或行政边界、实际控制线、冲突前线、武装组织控制区的专用数据集或分析 Overlay，也不会声称能从普通地图要素推断出这些数据的权威版本。这是对官方服务内容的约束，不是对任何领土法律地位的判断。用户可以在遵守 `LICENSE` 和第三方条款的前提下，自行研究合法取得或自行提供的数据；GeoDolly 不承诺内置工具能够抓取、推断或渲染此类敏感数据集。

### 外部数据、署名与留存

GeoDolly 可查询与 OpenStreetMap 相关的地点和地图要素服务，并从已配置的提供商加载栅格底图。OpenStreetMap 数据依据开放数据库许可证（ODbL）提供；底图影像、提供商元数据及其他外部数据可能适用独立条款。GeoDolly 的 `LICENSE` 不会重新许可这些内容，也不会免除署名义务。第三方瓦片和上游记录自身可能显示争议地名或边界；其出现不代表 GeoDolly 官方对此作出认定或背书。

地图页面、截图、导出内容及再分发的结果须保留数据和瓦片提供商要求的署名。使用者和部署者须遵守提供商条款、限流及适用的访问和缓存规则，不得规避访问控制或隐藏必须保留的署名。参见 [OpenStreetMap 版权说明](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)、[OpenStreetMap 瓦片使用政策](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)以及所配置提供商的条款。

外部地点、要素和瓦片请求会向已配置的提供商发送请求所需的信息，例如搜索词、地图范围或瓦片坐标；提供商自身的隐私与使用条款适用。自行托管的运营者决定提供商选择、访问日志和留存方式。官方托管服务在公开上线前需要另行提供隐私及留存说明；本文件不构成该声明。

### 官方服务控制

自行托管或修改的副本并非 GeoDolly 官方服务。如未来上线官方服务，运营方可以在公开仓库之外维护私有的 Core Policy、Restricted Data Config、Jurisdiction Policy 和 Official Internal Config，并在服务器侧代码中执行服务限制。用户可修改的公开 Prompt 和配置不能作为安全边界。本段描述计划中的分离方式，不表示私有控制已经部署。软件副本独立受到 `LICENSE` 约束，不依赖托管服务条款。
